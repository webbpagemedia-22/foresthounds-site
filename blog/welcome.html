Welcome to the Forest Hounds blog

Hello, and welcome. I'm David, and this is where I'll share what goes on during Forest Hounds walks around the Forest of Dean and the Wye Valley.

What I'll write about

  • Walks worth knowing. Routes I like around Speech House, Parkend, Coleford and the rest of the area, and which ones suit which dogs.
  • Training tips. Recall, loose-lead walking and manners: the things we practise walk after walk.
  • Seasonal notes. Mud, ticks, hot days and short winter afternoons, and what they mean for your dog.
  • News. When walk slots open up, and anything new at Forest Hounds.

If there's something you'd like me to write about, email me at hello@foresthounds.co.uk.
