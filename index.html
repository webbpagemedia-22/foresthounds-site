How a two-hour slot works

Every booking is a two-hour slot for your household's dogs alone. Travel is inside the slot, so the walk itself is a full hour.

1 Pick-up, about 30 minutes

I collect your dog from home and drive to a walk that suits them: open forest, riverside or quiet lanes.

2 The walk, one hour

A full hour out, on or off lead depending on your dog's recall. Any training goals get worked on as we go.

3 Home, about 30 minutes

Back home, towelled off, fresh water down, and a message to you on how it went.

Why one dog at a time

Group walks often mean four or more dogs at once. I don't do that. Your dog gets my full attention for the whole hour, and I do no more than three walks a day, four or five days a week.

  • Full attention

    No juggling leads. I'm watching your dog, not a pack.

  • Suits nervous and reactive dogs

    No strange dogs in the car or on the walk, so there's nothing to set them off.

  • Training that sticks

    Recall, loose-lead walking and manners, practised the same way walk after walk.

  • The same person every time

    Your dog gets to know me, and I get to know your dog.

About me

I'm David. Forest Hounds started because of Lyra, my own dog, who walked these woods with me for years. When she died I didn't want to replace her, but I didn't want to stop spending my days in the Forest with a dog at my side either. So now I walk yours.

I've spent my working life in hospitality and events management, looking after people, plans and safety. Turning up on time and doing what I said I'd do is the job.
